`version = "${file:VERSION}"`, and the release workflow checks both against the tag.

## Source

- **Rust oracle**: 4,454 library code lines in `rust-old/src/` (14 modules plus `integration/soorat.rs`, 102 `pub fn`s
  plus 65 trait methods), and the `oracle` hooks. Frozen; never edited.
- **Cyrius**: `[lib]` is, in order:
  - `src/error.cyr`;
  - the generated `src/constants.cyr` (112 constants);
  - the vendored `src/crmath_data.cyr` and `src/crmath.cyr` (kimiya 2.0.1's correctly rounded primitives: 58
    functions, 59 tables), and `src/crmath_ext.cyr` (pramana's correctly rounded log1p, tan and log2);
  - `src/mathx.cyr` (the Rust f64 surface);
  - the vendored `src/fmtf.cyr` (impetus 2.0.0's serde_json f64 printer, 15 items);
  - `src/pool.cyr` (the work-table pool and the bump allocator);
  - `src/json.cyr` (the JSON core: the pre-pass, the document arena, the readers and writers, the error codec);
  - `src/debug.cyr` (the derived Debug's builders, and `PramanaError`'s Debug);
  - `src/rng.cyr` (the `PmnRng` trait and `PmnSimpleRng`, with its JSON codec);
  - `src/combinatorics.cyr` (`factorial`, `permutations`, `combinations` into a `PmnU128`, and `stirling_approx`);
  - `src/bridge.cyr` (the 11 bridge functions);
  - `src/crerf_data.cyr` and `src/crerf.cyr` (CORE-MATH's erf and erfc, correctly rounded, without FMA; the tables
    generated by `scripts/vendor_coremath.py`);
  - `src/dd.cyr` (the double-double arithmetic the T1 special functions share);
  - `src/lgamma_data.cyr` and `src/lgamma.cyr` (lnΓ within 2 ulp, fdlibm's layout in double-double; the coefficients
    generated by `scripts/vendor_lgamma.py`);
  - `src/zeta_data.cyr` and `src/gammainc.cyr` (the incomplete gamma within 4 ulp, Rust's expansions in double-double;
    γ and ζ(k) generated by `scripts/gen_zeta.py`);
  - `src/betainc.cyr` (the incomplete beta within 8 ulp, Rust's continued fraction in double-double, with TOMS 708's
    power series and brcomp prefactor);
  - `src/special.cyr` (the special functions: all of math.rs, at T0 but for the T1 functions: erf and erfc since
    part b1, Φ and 1 − Φ since part b2, on src/crerf.cyr's double-double erfc, lnΓ since part b3, on
    src/lgamma.cyr, the incomplete gamma since part b4, on src/gammainc.cyr, and the incomplete beta since part b5, on src/betainc.cyr).

  They are bundled as `dist/pramana.cyr`. `src/main.cyr` is the scaffold's build entry.

## Upstream requests and stand-ins

The conversion ledger is the port plan's §10: 14 rows (L1–L14), all open, each naming its upstream request, and four
requests filed with no stand-in. `python3 -I -B scripts/upstream_ledger.py --check` gates it (CI too): 18 `UPSTREAM`
notes in `src/` so far, 0 failures. L13 (M1 part d3a) is the JSON reader's guard against `vec_push_a`'s capacity
abort, added to the open cyrius vec issue; L14 (part d4) the JSON writers' reservations before the stdlib writers
that abort on a failed grow, added to the open cyrius str_builder issue. The rest of the stand-ins arrive with M1–M7.
